Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Y Player (package yacine.yplayer) handles information, including Google user data.

Information we access

  • Local media: audio files and metadata on your device, used to build your library. This stays on your device.
  • Google account (optional): if you choose to connect, Y Player receives your basic account identity (name, email, profile photo) from Google Sign-In and a read-only authorization for your YouTube data.
  • YouTube data: your playlists and the videos in them (titles, channel names, thumbnails, durations, IDs), plus search results.

How we use it

Google user data is used only to display your playlists and play your selected videos inside Y Player. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

YouTube API Services

Y Player uses YouTube API Services. By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Google user data and scopes

Y Player requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly only. It cannot edit, upload, or delete anything on your account. Y Player's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

  • Google authorization tokens and YouTube data are handled on your device and are not sent to servers operated by Y Player.
  • We do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties, except with Google/YouTube as needed to provide the features you request.
  • Embedded YouTube playback is provided by YouTube and may collect data under its own policies.

Data protection and security

We protect the sensitive Google user data (your Google account identity, OAuth tokens and YouTube data) with the following measures:

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the app and Google/YouTube APIs uses HTTPS (TLS). Y Player does not send this data over unencrypted connections.
  • Secure storage on your device: OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore system, and are not kept in plain text. YouTube data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and Android app sandboxing applies.
  • No developer-side copy: Y Player does not operate servers that receive or store your Google user data, so there is no central database of tokens or YouTube data that could be breached.
  • Data minimization and least privilege: we request only the read-only youtube.readonly scope, and only after you choose to connect. We do not request write, upload, or delete permissions.
  • Restricted access: Google user data is accessible only to the app on your device and is not accessible to our team, to third parties, or to advertising or analytics services. Y Player does not include advertising SDKs.
  • No human access: no person reads your Google user data, because it never leaves your device except for requests to Google/YouTube.
  • Token removal: when you disconnect your account in the app, the stored tokens and cached YouTube data are deleted from your device. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions.
  • Retention: Google user data is kept only while your account is connected and is removed on disconnect, on clearing app data, or on uninstall.
  • Security updates and incidents: we keep dependencies up to date, and if we learn of a security issue affecting your data we will act promptly and notify affected users as required by applicable law. You can report security concerns to soussiyacine03@gmail.com.

Download and extraction

Y Player does not download YouTube videos or audio, and does not play YouTube content in the background or while the player is hidden.

Your choices

  • Disconnect: use the disconnect option in the YouTube section of the app.
  • Revoke access: visit your Google Account permissions.
  • Delete data: clearing app storage or uninstalling removes locally stored data.

Other services

Optional discovery sources (e.g. SoundCloud, Archive.org) receive the search requests you make to them and are governed by their own policies.

Children

Y Player is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
