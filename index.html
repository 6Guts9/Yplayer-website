Y Player

Y Player is a free Android music player for your local library, with discovery from sources such as SoundCloud and Archive.org, and an optional YouTube section.

Download from GitHub Releases

Features

  • Local music library and playlists
  • Full player and mini player, queue, shuffle and repeat
  • Discovery and search
  • Background audio for local and supported sources

YouTube integration

Y Player uses YouTube API Services. Users may optionally connect a Google account to search YouTube and browse and play their own YouTube and YouTube Music playlists.

  • Access is read-only, requested only after you choose to connect (scope: youtube.readonly).
  • Videos play in the official, visible YouTube embedded player.
  • Y Player does not download YouTube content or extract audio.
  • You can disconnect your account at any time inside the app.

By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
